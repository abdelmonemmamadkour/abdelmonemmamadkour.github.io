🎓 Privacy Policy — Dars Khosousy (Private Tutor AI)

Google Play Compliant   Effective Date: November 2026  |  Package Name: tech.madkour.darskhosousy  |  Developer: Madkour Tech

Welcome to "Dars Khosousy" (Private Tutor AI). We take the privacy of students, parents, and educators very seriously and are fully committed to complying with Google Play Developer Policies, COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act), and GDPR-K regulations.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

  • Study Questions & Prompts: Text prompts and curriculum questions entered by the student are transmitted directly to Google Gemini AI servers solely to generate pedagogical, step-by-step educational explanations and model solutions.
  • Homework Photos & Camera Access: When you snap a photo of a textbook exercise or homework assignment, the image is transmitted securely to interpret the problem. We do not archive or store your homework photos on external servers.
  • Local On-Device Storage (LocalStorage): All student preferences (selected country, academic stage, daily question quota, and your personal API key in BYOK mode) are stored strictly locally on your device. They are never transmitted to our private servers.
  • Microphone & Audio Dictation: Microphone permissions are used exclusively when you tap the voice dictation button to convert your spoken query into text using the device's native speech recognition engine.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the application and all national curricula 100% free for students worldwide, the app integrates family-safe Google AdMob advertising:

  • Google AdMob: Google may use device advertising identifiers to serve non-intrusive, family-safe ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Optional Rewarded Ads: Ads are watched strictly upon user consent when seeking extra daily questions or exporting study notes as a PDF.

3. Children's Privacy & COPPA Compliance

Dars Khosousy is engineered as a secure educational environment suitable for all ages:

  • We do not require account registration, personal phone numbers, or social media logins.
  • We never collect or sell Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from minors.
  • Ad inventory is configured to adhere to Google Families self-certified policies.

4. Data Retention and User Deletion Rights

Because all interaction history and settings are maintained on-device, you maintain total control over your data:

  • You can clear your personal API key anytime by tapping "Clear" in the Quota screen.
  • Clearing the app's cache/storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local records instantaneously.
